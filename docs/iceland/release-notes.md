# Bifröst Iceland release notes

This page is for the people who decide on the app and run it. It lists the versions of Bifröst Iceland published on Microsoft AppSource, newest first. To see the version installed in your company, open **Extension Management** in Business Central.

## 28.1.0 — 21 October 2026

### First version on Microsoft AppSource

- Look people and companies up in the national registry by kennitala, name or address, and keep a local copy of the registry in Business Central.
- File tax returns with Skatturinn: VAT, payroll withholding and capital income tax. Validate a return, submit it and get the receipt as a PDF.
- Preview the VAT statement per box number and run Business Central's VAT settlement.
- Bring exchange rates and other rates from Seðlabanki Íslands into Business Central.
- Send contribution returns through Skilagrein, check details on island.is, use Icelandic reference data, and send SMS.
- Start with Skatturinn's test environment. Credentials stay in protected storage, and separate permission sets let you give each filing to the people who may send it.
