# What you can do with Bifröst

*For people who use Business Central at work.*

Bifröst connects Business Central to your chat. These are cases that work today, grouped by role.
Choose your role, open a case, copy the prompt and paste it into the chat. Most answers come within
a minute. Cases that span several companies or a full close take longer, and the card says so.

## Start here {#start-here}

Open a new chat and first confirm which company you are connected to. Paste this:

<CopyPrompt text="Connect to Business Central with the Bifröst connector and tell me which company and environment is selected." />

You need the Bifröst connector switched on and access to Business Central; if that is not done yet,
follow [Try it out](/try-it-out/). Bifröst works with your own Business Central
permissions: what you may not do yourself, it cannot do either.

The first connection can take a moment. If the answer says Business Central cannot be reached, wait
a minute and paste the prompt again. Use one task per chat: when you change topic, start a new chat.

## Three levels {#three-levels}

- **Ask.** You ask, the system fetches the answer from Business Central and shows it in the chat.
- **Do.** The system does the work: it creates drafts, matches lines or posts when you ask it to.
- **Watch.** Questions worth asking regularly, to see what nobody was looking for.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- **The labels matter.** *Read only* changes nothing. *Creates drafts* creates unposted documents
  that you can throw away. *Writes to BC* changes data, and some cases post when you ask.
- **System rules still apply.** Permissions, closed periods and setup work as in the interface. What
  you may not do in Business Central does not happen in the chat either.
- **The cases send nothing out.** Letters and emails made here are drafts that you send yourself.
  Bifröst can send invoices by email, but that has to be set up separately.
- **Check figures you pass on.** The answer is worked out from the data as it stands that day. You
  are responsible for what you send out.
- **Bifröst cannot delete drafts.** Drafts that should not stand have to be deleted in Business
  Central.
