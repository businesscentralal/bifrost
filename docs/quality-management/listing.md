# Bifrost Quality Management

## Search result summary

Run quality inspections and handle inspected goods from an assistant or integration.

## Offer summary

Bifröst Quality Management connects an assistant or integration to Microsoft's Quality Management app in Business Central. Create inspections, record test values, finish or reopen inspections, arrange re-inspections and handle inspected goods using the standard Business Central processes.

## Full description

Quality teams can carry an inspection from its source record through measurements and completion without leaving the assistant they already use. The results remain visible on the standard Quality Management pages in Business Central.

Choose an inspection template or use configured generation rules. Record a test value and let Business Central validate it and recalculate the result. Finish the inspection when the required checks are satisfied, reopen it with the appropriate permissions, or create a re-inspection that preserves its history.

Use passed or failed quantities to prepare inventory movements or post a reclassification when requested. For failed goods from a purchase inspection, create an unposted purchase return order and review it through the usual purchasing process.

The app requires Bifröst Foundation, Microsoft's Quality Management app and the Business Central permissions for the work being performed. Inventory posting changes stock; review the intended quantities and destination before confirming. Creating a purchase return does not post it. Repeating a successful request can create additional inspections, movements or returns.
