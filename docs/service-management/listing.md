# Bifrost Service Management

## Search result summary

Run service orders, posting and service contracts from an assistant or integration.

## Offer summary

Bifröst Service Management connects an assistant or integration to Service Management in Business Central. Release and reopen service orders, allocate resources, convert quotes, post and undo service work, lend loaners and run service contract billing with the standard Business Central processes.

## Full description

Service teams can carry service work from quote to invoice without leaving the assistant they already use. The results remain visible on the standard Service Management pages in Business Central.

Release or reopen a service order, allocate a resource or resource group to a service item line and convert a service quote into an order. Ask for suggested resources, ranked by skills, service zone and free hours, or for suggested fault resolutions. Adjust prices from a service price group, with a preview and a reset.

Preview a posting before you post. Ship, invoice, ship and invoice, or ship and consume a service order, bill shipments on a standalone invoice or credit memo, and undo a posted shipment or consumption when Business Central allows it. Lend loaner equipment and receive it back with the loan history kept.

For service contracts, convert a contract quote, sign, lock and unlock a contract, and create invoices, service orders or a credit memo for a bounded set of contracts. The documents are created unposted, and a failed run is rolled back as a whole.

The app requires Bifröst Foundation, Business Central's Service Management with a Premium license, and the permissions for the work being performed. Posting and undoing change your books and stock; review the intended result before confirming. Repeating a successful request can create additional documents.
