## Full description text

**Bifrost Projects** brings project work in Business Central to the assistant your people already use.
An assistant, a planning tool or an integration can set up projects with their tasks and planning lines,
keep the plan current, post usage and work in progress, show where a project stands against its budget
and send the quote. Employees keep their time sheets from their assistant, approvers approve them there,
and field technicians record their visits, travel, kilometres and parts on site. Every step is a complete
Business Central task, built on Business Central's own project logic, and runs with the user's own
permissions.

### Who is this for?

**Project-based businesses that run their projects in Business Central**: project managers and
controllers, the people who keep and approve time sheets, the back office that invoices project work, and
service companies whose technicians work at customer sites.

**Target industries:** Professional services, engineering and installation, construction trades and
field service.

### What it does

- **Projects and planning** — Create a project with its tasks and planning lines in one step, update it,
  change its status, move planning dates, indent the task structure, copy projects and tasks, and archive
  versions.
- **Purchasing and usage** — Create purchase orders for the items a project still needs, link purchase
  lines to project tasks, move planning lines to the project journal and post item usage.
- **WIP and figures** — Calculate, post and reverse work in progress, maintain WIP methods, update item
  cost after a cost adjustment, and read budget against actual figures, statistics and the standard
  project reports as data or PDF.
- **Quotes** — Send a project quote to the customer through the document sending profile.
- **Resource planning** — Capacity, allocation to open projects and availability by period.
- **Time sheets** — Employees create, fill in and submit their own time sheets. The approver on each time
  sheet approves or rejects the lines addressed to them. Administrators create time sheets for a period,
  fill them from planning, register absence, reopen, post and archive.
- **Usage on time sheets** — Items and resources used on a job are recorded on the time sheet, approved
  with it and moved to the item, resource or project journal for posting.
- **Field service visits** — Technicians see their day, start and stop travel and work on a task, record
  kilometres and parts from their van, get the customer's signature on a visit report without prices, and
  ask for follow-up work that the project manager converts into project tasks.
- **Time Sheet Date on invoices** — Sales invoice and credit memo lines created from a project show the
  date of the work they invoice.

### How it works

1. Install Bifrost Foundation and Bifrost Projects, and connect your assistant to Bifröst.
2. Check **Projects Setup** and assign the permission sets for project work, time sheets and field
   service.
3. Ask your assistant to do the work: "Create a project for the new office fit-out with three tasks",
   "Show me the time sheet lines waiting for my approval", "Start work on task 1000 of project P-1020".
4. The results appear on the standard Business Central pages, and every call is logged in Business
   Central.
