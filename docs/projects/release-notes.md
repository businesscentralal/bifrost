# Bifröst Projects release notes

This page is for the people who decide on the app and run it. It lists the versions of Bifröst Projects published on Microsoft AppSource, newest first. To see the version installed in your company, open **Extension Management** in Business Central.

## 28.1.1 — 27 November 2026

### First version on Microsoft AppSource

- **Run projects from your assistant.** Create a project with its tasks and planning lines in one step, keep it current, change its status, move planning dates, copy projects and tasks, and archive versions, all with Business Central's own project logic.
- **Buy, use and post.** Create purchase orders for what a project still needs, link purchase lines to project tasks, move planning lines to the project journal, post item usage, and calculate, post and reverse work in progress.
- **Know where a project stands.** Budget against actual figures per task, project statistics, the standard project reports as data or PDF, and resource capacity and allocation by period.
- **Send the quote** of a project to the customer through the document sending profile.
- **Time sheets from the first hour to posting.** Employees create, fill in and submit their own time sheets from their assistant. The approver named on each time sheet approves or rejects the lines addressed to them, and nobody approves their own. Administrators create time sheets for a period, fill them from planning, register absence, reopen, post and archive.
- **Usage on time sheets.** Items and resources used on a job, such as parts and kilometres, are recorded on the time sheet, approved with it and moved to the item, resource or project journal for posting. Follow them on **Time Sheet Usage Lines**.
- **Field service visits.** Technicians see their day, start and stop travel and work on a project task, and the rounded hours go to their time sheet. They record kilometres and the parts taken from their van, get the customer's signature on a visit report without prices, and ask for follow-up work.
- **Follow-up requests.** The project manager converts a technician's request into a new or existing project task with planning lines, or rejects it. Requests and decisions are shown on **Project Follow-up Requests**.
- **Time Sheet Date on invoices.** Sales invoice and credit memo lines created from a project show the date of the work they invoice.
- **Set up in a few places.** **Projects Setup** turns the project operations on or off for a company and holds the field service settings. **Technician Setup** links each technician to their van. Permission sets for employees, approvers, posting, time sheet administration, usage and field technicians keep each role to its own work.
- **English and Icelandic** throughout, with help for every page.
