## Full description text

Bifrost opens up BC for agents, applications and integrations. It lets AI do real work inside Microsoft
Dynamics 365 Business Central, not just answer questions about it. It is an AI connector that links your
Business Central data, processes and business logic to Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and any other AI
platform that supports MCP (Model Context Protocol), through an app inside Business Central and a secure MCP
server.

### Who it is for

Business Central customers, partners and ISVs who want agentic AI in day-to-day ERP work without building
custom integrations.

### What Bifrost does

- **Works with the AI you choose:** Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot or any MCP-compatible platform, so you
  are not locked into one provider.
- **Reads and acts:** AI can look up information across Business Central and conduct analysis on it,
  furthermore it can carry out approved actions, such as creating sales documents, posting invoices.
- **Grows with add-on apps:** Bifrost add-ons bring more capabilities, such as direct connections to
  Icelandic banks, document exchange, storage, scheduled routines and language models. They work together:
  fetch the statement from the bank, reconcile it, and do it every morning.
- **Extends to your own solution:** ISVs and partners add their own capabilities for their extensions, and
  every connected AI can use them the day they are installed.
- **Keeps your security:** AI works within Business Central's own permissions. Administrators can restrict
  fields and see every call made through Bifrost.

### Typical uses

- Reports and dashboards built and adjusted by asking in plain language
- Help with data migration from older NAV or Business Central versions and other ERP systems
- Faster work for Business Central teams: demo data, exploring data, troubleshooting and configuration
- Automated testing of new and updated features, and before each release
- Allowing for automation of financial processes with agentic workflows
- AI services that partners build, package and resell to their customers

### Requirements and pricing

- An active Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central environment.
- **Free trial:** when the setup wizard is finished in a production environment, Bifrost starts a trial of
  1,000 user messages and 1,000 app registration messages, once per Microsoft Entra tenant.
- For prices, contact your Business Central partner. If you are a partner, contact The App Channel.

Bifrost does not replace Business Central or its extensions. It makes their data and business logic
available to the AI platforms your organisation already uses.
