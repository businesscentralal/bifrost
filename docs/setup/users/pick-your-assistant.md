# Pick your assistant

*For each person who will use Bifröst, for example someone in finance or sales. Your company has
already set up Bifröst in Business Central ([For your company](/setup/)); this is your part, and it
takes about ten minutes.*

## Which assistant

Bifröst works with the AI assistant you already use. Your company usually decides which one:

| Assistant | How it connects |
|---|---|
| **Claude** (on the web or [Claude Desktop](https://claude.ai/download)) | A connector you add yourself; step by step in [Connect your assistant](/setup/connect-your-ai/#claude) |
| **Microsoft Copilot** | Not in its store yet; until then, as a remote MCP server where your Copilot allows it ([how](/setup/connect-your-ai/#microsoft-copilot)) |
| **ChatGPT** | Not in its store yet; until then, as a remote MCP server where your ChatGPT allows it ([how](/setup/connect-your-ai/#chatgpt)) |
| **Another assistant** | Any assistant that supports remote MCP servers |

## What you need first

- **An account with the assistant**, and in a company workspace, permission to add a connector.
  Some companies let only the workspace owner add connectors; ask your administrator.
- **Your Business Central user**, with the Bifröst permissions your administrator has given you.
  The assistant works as you, with exactly your rights.
- **The MCP server address**, from your administrator. You paste it in when you add the connector.
