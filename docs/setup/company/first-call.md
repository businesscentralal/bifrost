# Step 5: Check it and invite your users

**Who is needed:** The Business Central administrator.

1. **Check the setup.** Bifrost Setup opens without a setup notification at the top, and every caller has
   the permissions from [step 2](/setup/business-central/#give-people-and-apps-permission), with
   only the posting gates it needs.
2. **Connect yourself first.** Go through [For each user](/setup/pick-your-assistant/) as the first
   user, and ask *"Who am I in Business Central?"*. The call appears on **Bifrost Messages**, with you
   as the caller.
3. **Try something real.** [Try it out](/try-it-out/) has prompts to start with, from a simple
   question to a change you preview first.
4. **Invite your users.** Send each user:
   - which assistant your company uses, and the **MCP server address** from
     [step 3](/setup/consent/#what-you-get-from-the-setup-wizard);
   - the link to [For each user](/setup/pick-your-assistant/), where they connect their assistant
     and ask their first question;
   - the **Connection Prompt** from Bifrost Setup (*Environment* section) for each company they work in.
     Users with only `BIFROST API ori` may not be able to open Bifrost Setup themselves;
   - [Using Bifröst](/documentation/end-customers/users/): what they can ask, what it will not do,
     and what to do when it says no.

**Start small.** Invite a few users first, with `BIFROST API ori` only, so the assistant can answer and prepare
but not post. Look at **Bifrost Messages** after a week, then give posting gates to the few who should post through
an assistant, and invite the rest.

That is the company's setup done. From here on, see
[Running Bifröst: for administrators](/documentation/end-customers/administrators/).
