title: "Select File Account"
sidebar_position: 4
---

**Select File Account** lists the Business Central file accounts for the connector selected on the
[storage connection card](/help/attachments/storage-card/).

Select the account you want and confirm. Its name then appears on the connection card. Cancelling
leaves the selection unchanged.

If no account appears, check that the connector is correct and register a matching account through
**Storage Setup Wizard** on [Bifrost Attachments Setup](/help/attachments/attachments-setup/). Manage
account credentials in Business Central's file account setup, rather than on the connection card.
