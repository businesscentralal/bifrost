# Bifröst Iceland Treasury — Help

In-product help for **Bifrost Iceland Treasury**. Business Central opens these pages from the help icon on each page of the app.

The app connects Business Central to five Icelandic banks — Landsbankinn, Arion banki, Íslandsbanki, Kvika banki and Sparisjóðir — for claims, payments, statements, cards, currency rates and electronic documents.

## Setting up

- **[Bifrost Iceland Treasury Setup](./treasury-setup.md)** — the one page where every bank connector is configured.
- **[Treasury Setup Wizard](./treasury-setup-wizard.md)** — guided setup, one bank at a time.
- **[Bank secrets](./treasury-secrets.md)** — what credentials each bank needs, where they are stored, and why they do not migrate from the older apps.
