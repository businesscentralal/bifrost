The **Companies** lookup lists the companies you can filter the [Bifrost Usage Entries](/help/foundation/license-usage/)
page by: your own companies, or the companies of the customer, Partner or Vendor view you opened the
usage page from.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Company Name** | The company name. |
| **Environment Name** | The Business Central environment the company runs in. |
| **Environment Type** | **Production**, **Sandbox** or **OnPremises**. |

Select a company and choose **OK** to filter the usage entries by it.
