The **Cancel - Reason** dialog opens when you cancel a Partner or a Customer, or reject a leave
request. Enter an optional reason - it travels with the cancellation or the rejection and is shown to
the other tenant on its Bifröst Setup page after its next Sync. Leave it blank to skip.

See [Leaving and cancelling](/foundation/licensing/leaving-and-cancelling/).
