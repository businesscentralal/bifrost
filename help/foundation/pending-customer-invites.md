title: "Pending Customer Invites"
---

**Pending Customer Invites** lists the invitations of more than one Partner to your tenant. The
**Register as Customer** notification now opens the
[Bifrost Customer Registration](/help/foundation/customer-registration-wizard/) wizard, whose Partner step lists
the same invitations. Choose the Partner you want to work with.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Friendly Name** | The name the Partner gave your tenant. |
| **Partner Tenant Id Hash** | The Partner that sent the invitation. |
| **Invited At** | When the invitation was sent (UTC). |

Select a row and choose **Accept**. Your tenant then moves to the **Subscription** license and the
Partner invoices your usage. Only a licence administrator can accept an invitation.

See [License types](/licensing/license-types/#subscription).
