**Pending Customer Invites** opens when you choose **Register as Customer** and more than one Partner
has invited your tenant. Choose the Partner you want to work with.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Friendly Name** | The name the Partner gave your tenant. |
| **Partner Tenant Id Hash** | The Partner that sent the invitation. |
| **Invited At** | When the invitation was sent (UTC). |

Select a row and choose **Accept**. Your tenant then moves to the **Subscription** license and the
Partner invoices your usage. Only a licence administrator can accept an invitation.

See [Being a Customer](/foundation/licensing/customer/).
