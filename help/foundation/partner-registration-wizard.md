title: "Bifrost Partner Registration"
---

The **Bifrost Partner Registration** wizard registers the current tenant as a Bifröst **Partner** of the
Vendor that invited it. It starts only from a **Sync** on the [Bifrost Setup](/help/foundation/bifrost-setup/)
page: choose **Register as Partner** on the notification that Sync raises when a Vendor has invited the tenant.
Opened any other way, for example by URL, the wizard refuses to open. It is available only to users with licence
administration permission, once the End-User License Agreement is approved for the company, and only when exactly
one Partner invitation is pending. A sandbox cannot register.

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Review and edit company information** | The [company step](/help/foundation/registration-company-information/): name, address, post code, city, county, country/region, phone number, email and VAT registration number, filled in from **Company Information** - what your Vendor and Origo see for the Partner. Correct them here; the name is required. |
| **2. Confirm the Partner registration** | Names the Vendor that invited the tenant, by its registered company name, and the company you register. |

Choose **Finish** to register with the details of the company step: the invitation is accepted and the whole
tenant becomes a Partner of that Vendor. Your changes go only into the Partner registration, and Company
Information is not changed. **Customer Management** then appears on Bifröst Setup. **Cancel** registers nothing.
