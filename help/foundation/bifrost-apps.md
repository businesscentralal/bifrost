title: "Bifröst Apps"
---

**Find Apps** is in the **Apps** group on [Bifrost Setup](/help/foundation/bifrost-setup/). It opens the Bifröst app registry on the web, in your browser.

The registry lists every Bifröst app with a short summary, its AppSource status, and links to AppSource, the documentation and the release notes.

To see which Bifröst apps and versions are installed in this environment, open **Extension Management**. Administrators in online environments also get the **Bifröst App Updates** list, described on the [Bifrost Applications](/help/foundation/registered-apps/) page, when a newer version of an installed Bifröst app is on AppSource.

Find Apps does not install or update apps. Install from AppSource, and update in the Business Central admin center under **Environments**, **Apps**.
