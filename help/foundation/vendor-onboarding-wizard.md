The **Bifrost Vendor Onboarding** wizard registers the current tenant as a Bifröst **Vendor**. It is
available only to a tenant that Origo has approved as a Vendor, and only to users with licence
administration permission; it opens from **Onboard as Vendor** on the [Bifrost Setup](/help/foundation/bifrost-setup/)
page or from the *Register as Vendor* notification.

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Vendor onboarding** | Shows your Microsoft Entra tenant ID and whether the tenant is already registered. |
| **2. Review Company Information** | Shows the name, address, city, country/region, phone number, email and VAT registration number from **Company Information** - what your Partners and Origo see for the Vendor. Correct Company Information first if needed. |

Choose **Finish** to register. **Partner Management**, **Customer Management**, **Pending Partner
Leave Requests** and **Deregister as Vendor** then appear on Bifröst Setup. Run the wizard again after
changing Company Information to refresh the registration.

See [Working as a Vendor](/foundation/licensing/vendor/).
